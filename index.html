About the platform

Plate & Place is a restaurant discovery and reservation platform built around three values: convenience, variety, and accessibility. Whether you're planning a quiet date night, a family lunch or a business dinner, you can browse curated venues, answer a few quick questions and get a personalised shortlist. Reservations include an optional refundable deposit, so restaurants know you're coming and you skip the queue.

Who we're for

Families

Find kid-friendly spots with relaxed seating, generous portions and dietary options that work for the whole group.

Couples & Date Nights

Discover intimate, atmospheric venues with signature dishes and a price range that fits the occasion.

Professionals & Tourists

Browse polished business-dining options and stand-out local venues - perfect for visiting clients or exploring a new city.
