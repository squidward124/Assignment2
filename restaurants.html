Bella Cucina

Italian

Bella Cucina is a warm, family-run trattoria in the heart of the city, specialising in regional Italian cuisine. Our chefs prepare hand-rolled pasta daily and fire authentic Neapolitan pizzas in a traditional wood oven imported from Naples. The dining room blends rustic stonework with modern touches, creating a relaxed setting that suits family dinners, casual dates and birthday celebrations. Vegetarian options are clearly marked, and our team can accommodate most dietary needs with advance notice.

Sakura Sushi

Japanese

Sakura Sushi brings the precision of Tokyo's intimate omakase counters to Melbourne. Our sushi chefs source premium-grade tuna, salmon and ocean-caught seafood, preparing each piece in front of guests at our 12-seat sushi bar. The omakase tasting menu features ten courses paired with sake or premium green tea. The wider dining room offers a quieter setting with seasonal small-plate menus, perfect for special occasions and business dinners. Halal-friendly options are available - just ask your server.

Spice Garden

Indian

Spice Garden is a vibrant North Indian kitchen run by a Punjabi family with three generations of restaurant experience. The menu showcases tandoor-grilled meats, slow-simmered curries and freshly baked naan from our clay oven. We grind our own masalas daily and source halal-certified meats from a local butcher. Vegetarian and vegan diners are well looked after, with around half the menu being plant-based. The bright, communal dining room works well for large family lunches and after-work group dinners.

  • Butter Chicken$22
  • Lamb Rogan Josh$26
  • Garlic Naan$6
Avg $25 - $35 / person Deposit $15
Reserve at Spice Garden

Green Leaf

Plant-Based

Green Leaf is a fully plant-based restaurant committed to bold, creative cooking that proves vegan food can be the star of the table. Our menu features grain bowls, jackfruit tacos, cashew-cream pastas and a signature beetroot burger that has won local awards two years running. We source produce from regenerative farms within 100 kilometres of the city, and every dish is also free of palm oil and refined sugar. The bright, plant-filled interior suits brunch, lunch or a date night.

  • Coconut Pumpkin Curry$24
Avg $22 - $32 / person Deposit $15
Reserve at Green Leaf

The Prime Cut

Steakhouse

The Prime Cut is an upscale modern steakhouse showcasing dry-aged beef from premium Australian producers, charcoal-grilled over Japanese binchotan coals. Cuts range from a classic Black Angus ribeye to grain-fed Wagyu graded MB7 and above. Sides are designed for sharing - truffle-mashed potato, charred broccolini and seasonal vegetable plates. The wine list is curated by our sommelier with a focus on Australian reds and aged Bordeaux. A natural choice for corporate dinners and milestone celebrations.

Olive & Vine

Mediterranean

Olive & Vine is a coastal Mediterranean restaurant inspired by the kitchens of Greece, Lebanon and southern Italy. Our menu centres on shared mezze plates, charcoal-grilled lamb and fresh seafood dressed simply with olive oil, lemon and herbs from our rooftop garden. The whitewashed dining room with terracotta floors and natural timber feels relaxed by day and intimate by night. Vegan and halal-friendly options are clearly marked, and our sommelier curates small-batch Greek and Italian wines.
